"We'd rather sell you one coat you wear for ten winters than ten coats you wear once."

Nostra started in a single workshop with a simple brief: make fewer things, and make them properly. Every fabric is chosen for how it ages, not just how it photographs — raw selvedge denim, brushed alpaca, waxed cotton canvas.

We work with the same small mills season after season, which means we know exactly where every garment comes from, and so will you.
